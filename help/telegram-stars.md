# I bought Telegram Stars, but they didn't arrive. What should I do?

Telegram Stars are Telegram's own currency, kept in your Stars balance on
Telegram.

1. **Check your Stars balance:** open **Settings → Your Stars**, signed in as
   the Telegram user you bought the Stars for.
2. **Stars from the App Store or Google Play:** ask the store that charged you
   for the Stars ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212),
   [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2850369)).
3. **Stars from a website or a bot:** ask the site or bot you bought the Stars
   from. On iPhone and iPad, Nicegram's Stars screen offers **Get Stars
   (Multichain & non-KYC)** (the NiceStars website), **Get on Fragment**
   (Fragment) and **Get on Telegram** (Telegram's @PremiumBot).
4. **Other Stars questions:** [Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq#telegram-support).

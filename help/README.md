# Help and safety

- [Feature not visible](feature-not-visible.md) — check platform, version, plan,
  region, rollout, and account state.
- [App problems](app-problems.md) — the app will not open or keeps loading, an
  update prompt with no update in the store, repeated notifications, battery use,
  a forgotten app passcode, and using a proxy.
- [Groups and channels](groups-and-channels.md) — why you can read a group but not
  write, the limit on joined groups and channels, and joins that keep loading.
- [Privacy and data](privacy-and-data.md) — understand Telegram data, Nicegram
  features, AI processing, analytics, and local storage.
- [Support and source code](support-and-source.md) — use official community,
  policies, and public repositories.

Lost your phone or SIM card? See
[I lost my phone or SIM card](../getting-started/accounts.md#i-lost-my-phone-or-sim-card).
Forgot your two-step verification password? See
[I forgot my two-step verification password](../getting-started/accounts.md#i-forgot-my-two-step-verification-password).
Want your own copy of your chats? See
[How do I back up all my chats and media?](../getting-started/accounts.md#how-do-i-back-up-all-my-chats-and-media)

For account compromise, first secure the Telegram account from a trusted Telegram
client: review active sessions, terminate unfamiliar sessions, and enable two-step
verification. Never send a login code or recovery secret to support.

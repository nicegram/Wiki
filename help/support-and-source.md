# Where can I get Nicegram support or inspect the source?

## Product and support

- Support by e-mail: [support@nicegram.me](mailto:support@nicegram.me)
- Refunds of Nicegram subscriptions, accounts or sessions bought in Nicegram, and unusual cases: a person at
  [@nicegram_support_manager](https://t.me/nicegram_support_manager).
- [Nicegram website](https://nicegram.me)
- [Download Nicegram](https://nicegram.me/download)
- [Nicegram FAQ](https://nicegram.me/faq)
- [Community chat](https://t.me/nicegramchat)
- [Official Telegram channel](https://t.me/nicegramapp)
- [Privacy Policy](https://nicegram.me/privacy-policy)
- [Terms of Use](https://nicegram.me/terms-of-use)

Community participants are not automatically Nicegram employees. Never share login
codes, two-step passwords, exported account sessions, private keys, recovery
material, or remote-access control of your device.

## Public source

- [Nicegram for iOS](https://github.com/nicegram/Nicegram-iOS)
- [Nicegram for Android](https://github.com/nicegram/Nicegram-Android)
- [Nicegram Desktop](https://github.com/nicegram/nicegram-desktop)
- [Nicegram organization](https://github.com/nicegram)

Nicegram is built from open-source Telegram clients and adds platform-specific
code. Some product integrations are not present in the public checkout, so public
source can prove that an integration point exists without proving that every part
of the feature is available or reproducible.

## Reporting a problem

Include:

1. platform and operating-system version;
2. Nicegram version;
3. affected feature and account type;
4. steps that reproduce the issue;
5. expected and actual result;
6. a redacted screenshot or log when safe.

For source-code issues, use the relevant repository’s issue tracker when enabled.
For account, billing, wallet, or private-data problems, write to
[support@nicegram.me](mailto:support@nicegram.me), also when the app does not open.

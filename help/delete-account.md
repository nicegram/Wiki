# How do I delete my account?

Nicegram uses your Telegram account, so first choose what you want to remove.

**Remove it only from Nicegram on this device:** log out. Your Telegram account
and its chats stay. Logging out ends its Secret Chats.

- **iPhone and iPad:** **Settings → Edit → Log Out**.
- **Android:** **Settings → ⋮ → Log out**.

**Delete your Telegram account for good:**

- On any device, use Telegram's
  [deactivation page](https://my.telegram.org/auth?to=delete). Telegram
  recommends a computer browser for it, and you confirm it in the Telegram app,
  not by SMS.
- In Nicegram on iPhone and iPad: **Settings → Privacy and Security → If Away
  For → Delete Account Now**.

Deleting cannot be undone. Your messages, contacts and other data are removed.
Groups and channels you created stay without a creator, and their admins keep
their rights. Your contacts keep their copies of the messages you sent them. If
you join Telegram again later, you start as a new user and don't get your
history, contacts or groups back.

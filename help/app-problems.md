# Nicegram app problems

Start with the steps that keep your data. Read
[Before you reinstall Nicegram](#before-you-reinstall-nicegram) before deleting the app.

## Nicegram won’t open, crashes, or keeps loading

1. Close Nicegram completely and open it again, then restart the device.
2. If the chat list shows **Connecting…** or **Waiting for network**, the app
   has no connection to Telegram yet. Check Wi-Fi or mobile data.
3. Install the newest version from your store or the
   [official download page](https://nicegram.me/download).
4. If the device is almost full, free some space.
5. Still failing? Report it through
   [Support and source code](support-and-source.md#reporting-a-problem) with the
   device, system version, Nicegram version, and what you see.

## Before you reinstall Nicegram

After a reinstall you sign in to each account again, so check that you can:
[the login code](https://telegram.org/faq#getting-a-code-via-telegram) often
arrives in Telegram on another signed-in device, and you need your two-step
password if you set one.

[Telegram keeps your cloud chats](https://telegram.org/faq#q-how-do-i-log-out).
Secret chats are lost, and local Nicegram data such as quick replies and
downloads may not come back.

## Nicegram asks me to update, but the store has no update

- **“This message is not supported on your version”** or the same about a story
  refers to that one item. The rest of the app keeps working.
- **“Please update Telegram to the latest version to log in”** is sent by
  Telegram’s servers during login.
- **The Nicegram Update screen** (iPhone and iPad) appears when Telegram’s
  servers report an update. **Update Nicegram** opens the App Store page;
  **Skip** is offered unless the update is marked as required.

If your store shows nothing newer, use the
[official download page](https://nicegram.me/download), which also links the
latest Android APK. If the prompt blocks the app, report it to support with your
Nicegram version and a screenshot of the prompt.

## Notifications repeat or keep coming back

1. In **Settings → Notifications and Sounds**
   ([Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq#notification-problems)), check that
   notifications are set the way you want, and mute chats you do not need.
2. With several accounts, **All Accounts** decides whether every signed-in
   account notifies you or only the active one.
3. Check the system notification settings for Nicegram.
4. Update Nicegram, then close and reopen it.

If it continues, report the platform, Nicegram version, and how often it happens.
Do not send message contents.

## Nicegram drains the battery

- [**Settings → Power Saving**](https://telegram.org/blog/power-saving) (called
  **Power Usage** on some Android builds) can switch on below a battery level you
  choose and turns off autoplay, animations, and effects.
- On Android, **Keep-Alive Service** and **Background Connection** in
  **Notifications and Sounds** keep the app running in the background for
  reliable notifications. Decide whether you need them.

If battery use stays high, report the device model, system version, and Nicegram
version.

## I forgot my app passcode and can’t unlock Nicegram

The app passcode is **Passcode Lock** in **Settings → Privacy and Security**.
Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint is an option inside it, so the
passcode still exists. It is not the two-step password you enter at sign-in.

There is no passcode reset. As the passcode screen says, if you forget it, you
need to delete and reinstall the app, and secret chats are lost. Wrong attempts
make the app wait before the next try. Read
[Before you reinstall Nicegram](#before-you-reinstall-nicegram) first.

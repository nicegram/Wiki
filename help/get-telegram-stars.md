# How do I get Telegram Stars?

Telegram Stars are Telegram's own currency. On iPhone and iPad, open
**Settings → Your Stars** and choose where to get Stars:

- **Get Stars (Multichain & non-KYC)** opens the NiceStars website;
- **Get on Fragment** opens Fragment;
- **Get on Telegram** opens Telegram's @PremiumBot.

On Android, Nicegram shows Telegram's own Stars screen.

# How can I advertise my channel, group or bot on Nicegram?

Use Nicegram Ads, Nicegram's own ad platform. It promotes a channel, group,
bot or project to Nicegram's audience.

- **In Telegram:** start [@NiceAdsBot](https://t.me/NiceAdsBot) and create a
  campaign there.
- **On the web:** go to [ads.nicegram.me](https://ads.nicegram.me).

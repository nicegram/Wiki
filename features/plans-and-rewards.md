# How do plans, Gems, and referrals work?

Nicegram can gate features through a subscription, a usage balance, or both. The
price and included features shown in your current store and purchase screen are
authoritative because plans differ by platform, country, currency, and time.

## Subscriptions

Supported builds can offer Premium or Premium+ access. Before confirming, review:

- billing period and local price;
- trial end date, if any;
- included AI or feature allowance;
- renewal terms and the store account that owns the purchase.

Cancel through the store or purchase channel that processed the subscription.
Cancellation normally stops renewal; it is not automatically a refund.

Nicegram Premium is separate from Telegram Premium. An eligible plan can include
an account-shop allowance, but this is not a universal benefit of every Premium
purchase. Check the displayed benefits and Telegram Sessions balance.

### Where do I see when my Nicegram Premium renews or ends?

A subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play is managed in that
store account's subscriptions list:

- **iPhone and iPad:** open the device's **Settings**, tap your name, then
  **Subscriptions**, and tap the subscription. An expiration message in red means
  it is already cancelled. Turn on **Renewal Receipt Emails** there to get a
  receipt each time a subscription renews.
- **Android:** open [subscriptions on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions),
  or the device's **Settings → Google →** your name **→ Manage your Google Account
  → Payments & subscriptions → Manage subscriptions**. After you cancel, you can
  still use the subscription for the time you have already paid.

Can't find it? It may be on another Apple or Google account: Apple suggests
searching your email for "receipt from Apple" to see which account paid. If you
paid another way, write to [support@nicegram.me](mailto:support@nicegram.me).

## Gems and usage balance

Gems or a related balance can represent credit for eligible AI and paid actions.
The visible balance, action cost, and purchase confirmation in the current app are
authoritative. Do not use old conversion tables from retired Wiki pages.

## Referrals and rewards

Referral links, campaign tasks, and historical balance views are separate from
new earning. Old scrolling, autopilot and referral earning guides are not current
instructions. An old guide or visible historical balance does not
prove that new rewards accrue or that a payout is due. Check the terms and status
of a specific current campaign.

The previous Scroll-to-Earn and Wallet-to-Earn guides were removed because they no
longer represented a reliable current user promise.

## I bought Telegram Stars, but they didn't arrive. What should I do?

Telegram Stars are Telegram's own currency, kept in a balance on your Telegram
account.

1. **Check the balance on the right account.** Open **Settings → Your Stars**
   in the account you bought the Stars for. With several accounts, check which
   one is active in the account switcher.
2. **Contact whoever took your payment.** Telegram's Stars terms say that
   problems with a Stars purchase go to the company that processed the payment.
   - **App Store or Google Play:** the purchase is in the purchase history of
     that store account ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212),
     [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2850369)), and
     your billing relationship for it is with the store.
   - **A website or a bot:** write to the support of the service you paid. On
     iPhone and iPad, Nicegram's Stars screen offers **Get Stars (Multichain &
     non-KYC)**, which opens the NiceStars website, **Get on Fragment**, which
     opens Fragment, and **Get on Telegram**, which opens Telegram's @PremiumBot.
3. **For anything else, contact Telegram Support:** in the official Telegram
   app, go to **Settings → Ask a Question**. Volunteers answer, so a reply can
   take a while. In Nicegram on iPhone and iPad, **Ask a Question** opens
   Nicegram's helper bot instead.

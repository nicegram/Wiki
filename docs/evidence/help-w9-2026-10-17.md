Stars section; sign-in and password questions shifted by the words account, password and receipt). These sourced
statements were removed to keep each section on its own question; their sources are unchanged.

| Removed statement | Why |
|---|---|
| Stars: “Contact whoever took your payment”, “your billing relationship for it is with the store”, “write to the support of the service you paid” | Generic billing and support wording; each step now names Stars |

| Premium: **Renewal Receipt Emails** | The word receipt |
| Premium: the Android device-settings path through **Manage your Google Account** | The word account; the Google Play subscriptions link is kept |
| Premium: “It may be on another Apple or Google account”, search email for “receipt from Apple” | The words account and receipt |
| Premium: “If you paid another way, write to `support@nicegram.me`” | Generic support wording |

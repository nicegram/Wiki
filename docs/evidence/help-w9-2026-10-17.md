# Help W9 evidence — October 17, 2026 (Stars, impersonation, Premium renewal)

This public ledger backs three sections written for user questions the helper bot could not answer on
October 17: “I bought Telegram Stars, but they didn't arrive. What should I do?” and “Where do I see when my
Nicegram Premium renews or ends?” on [Plans, Gems, and referrals](../../features/plans-and-rewards.md), and
“Someone is pretending to be me or someone else. How do I report it?” on
[Profiles, folders, and interface](../../features/profiles-and-folders.md). Nicegram source links are pinned to
the revisions reviewed on October 10 (iOS `f42e2ad`, which is still `origin/master` on October 17; Android
`235be7c`). A string in public source shows what a client can display; it does not prove that a given installed
release shows it. Official pages were fetched on October 17, 2026.
